That Saturday enquiry got a reply on Tuesday. By then they'd booked someone else.

Me² answers your customers' emails and messages in your voice, so enquiries get a reply while the customer is still deciding. A trained Me² customer service specialist owns the work, with AI agents set up for your business doing the drafting. A person checks every reply before it goes out.

## What we take on

**Customer emails and messages.** Questions about orders, bookings, services and prices answered from your own information, the way you'd answer them.

**New enquiries.** The details you need gathered, the enquiry logged in your CRM, and a hot lead passed to you or your sales team straight away.

**Order and booking updates.** Changes, delays and "where's my order?" questions handled from your systems.

**Refunds and complaints, with you in control.** Anything unusual comes to you first. You decide; we do the follow-through and let the customer know. Your approval limits are written into the process.

## Evenings and weekends included

Your customers don't only write between nine and five. We usually reply within 2 hours, evenings and weekends included. Our team is in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, three hours behind Sydney time (four during daylight saving), so an Australian evening is still our working day.

## In your voice, not ours

Before we answer a single customer, we write down how you talk to them: the words you use, the ones you don't, what you'll offer and what needs your yes. AI agents draft from that guide and your own information. Your Me² specialist checks every name, date and number against the source and makes sure it sounds like you. We don't put an unchecked AI in front of your customers.

## How it works

1. **Send us a short note** about where enquiries are slipping.
2. **We write your voice guide and processes** with you, including what needs your approval.
3. **We work in your tools.** Usually your inbox and your CRM, so you don't change software. If anything would need to change, we'll say so first.
4. **Your dedicated Me² person reports back** on what came in, what was resolved and what needs you.

We can start within 2 weeks of you saying yes.
