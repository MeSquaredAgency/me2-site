## Description: 500 words

Me² runs the back office of established Australian businesses: everything behind the front line, from admin and operations to customer service, finance, marketing, social media and paid ads. The work is done by trained people backed by AI agents built for each business, so the owner, the sales team and the front line can do what they're best at. We also design and build websites.

**A person you know.** Every client gets one Me² person assigned to their business and dedicated to them. They learn how the business likes things done, they're the one the owner messages when something comes up, and they report back like part of the team.

**Specialists in each field.** Behind that person, each kind of work is done by our specialist in it: our admin, finance, customer service and marketing executives. Nobody is expected to be brilliant at everything.

**People first, backed by AI.** AI agents set up around each business do the drafting and the volume. A Me² specialist checks every name, date and number against the source before anything reaches a customer, a supplier or the owner. We don't sell AI on its own.

**What we take on:**

- Business operations: client intake, quote follow-up and the processes that hold it together
- Admin: data entry, scheduling, filing and the inbox
- Customer service: customer emails and messages answered in the client's voice
- Finance and accounting: debtor follow-up, bill coding and weekly reporting
- Marketing: newsletters, website updates, enquiry follow-up and a monthly report
- Social media: posts drafted on schedule, for the client to approve
- Paid ads: campaigns set up, watched and reported on
- Websites and digital products: websites and landing pages, interface design for portals and apps, copy and content structure, and search and performance foundations

**Every process written down.** Every repeat task becomes a written process that belongs to the client, so nothing stops if someone is away, and nothing walks out the door.

**Straightforward terms.** The whole back office is one monthly fee, billed in advance. An extra set of hands is quoted per job, and websites per project. Every quote is built around what the business needs, and clients know the price before anything starts. Back-office work runs month to month, and either side can stop with 30 days' notice. Set-up is part of the quote and paid up front. Clients keep their data, their logins and their written processes. A website and its domain belong to the client once it's paid for.

**When we get it wrong, we own it.** We fix mistakes at no charge, tell the client what happened and update the process so it doesn't happen again.

**Data handled with care.** Only the Me² people doing a client's work can see its data. Each has their own login, only to the systems their job needs, and we remove it when the work ends. Client data is never used to train AI models.

**Where we are.** Our team works from our Command Centre in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, serving businesses across Australia. We usually reply within 2 hours, evenings and weekends included, and can start within 2 weeks of a client saying yes.

Start small with one job, or hand over the lot. Visit mesquaredagency.com or email hello@mesquaredagency.com.

(about 540 words. If a field caps at 500, drop the 'Data handled with care' paragraph.)
