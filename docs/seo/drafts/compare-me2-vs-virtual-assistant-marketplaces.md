# Me² or a freelance virtual assistant from a marketplace?

Freelance marketplaces have made it easy to find a virtual assistant anywhere in the world, often within a day. For plenty of businesses that's the right answer. For others it becomes one more person to manage. Here's an honest look at both, so you can choose.

## The short answer

- **Hire a freelancer** if you have one well-defined job, time to brief and check the work, and you're happy to be the manager.
- **Choose Me²** if you want the work done and checked without managing it yourself, with someone answerable when it goes wrong and cover when people are away.

## Where a freelancer wins

Let's be fair: marketplaces do some things very well.

**Speed and choice.** You can browse profiles, read ratings and hire someone within days.

**Flexibility.** Pay for a few hours, a one-off project, or a specialist skill you need once.

**Low commitment.** No contract to speak of. If it doesn't work out, you stop.

**Lower hourly rates, often.** If your own time is free and the task is simple, a freelancer can cost less.

If you need a one-off spreadsheet cleaned up, a logo resized, or a few hours of data entry, a freelancer is probably the sensible choice.

## Where it gets harder

The trouble usually starts when the work is ongoing and matters to your customers.

**You become the manager.** Someone has to brief the freelancer, answer their questions, check their work and chase it when it's late. That's usually you, and it's the time you were trying to free up.

**One person, every task.** A good general VA may not be a good bookkeeper, and a good bookkeeper may not write your newsletter. You end up hiring several freelancers and managing all of them.

**The process walks out the door.** When a freelancer moves on, what they knew about your business often goes with them. You start again.

**Nobody checks before it goes out.** If an invoice reminder goes to the wrong customer, you find out from the customer.

**Access sprawl.** Logins get shared and rarely removed. Over a few years, it's easy to lose track of who can still get into your systems.

## How Me² is different

**A person you know, and a team behind them.** Every client gets one dedicated Me² person as their main contact. They learn how you like things done and bring in our specialist in each kind of work, so the bookkeeping is done by a finance specialist and the newsletter by a marketing specialist.

**A person owns every task.** AI agents set up for your business do the drafting and the volume. Our specialist checks every name, date and number against the source before anything leaves. We don't sell AI on its own.

**Written down, and yours.** Every repeat task becomes a written process, so nothing stops if someone is away. If you leave, you keep it, along with your data and your logins.

**We own our mistakes.** You hear it from us, not your customers. We fix it at no charge and change the process.

**Clear terms.** Month to month, 30 days' notice either way. Set-up is part of your quote and paid up front. You'll know the price before anything starts. We usually reply within 2 hours, evenings and weekends included.

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Is this a one-off job or ongoing work?
2. Do I have time each week to brief and check someone's work?
3. Would a mistake reach my customers or my books?
4. What happens if this person leaves next month?
5. Do I need one skill, or several?

If you answered "ongoing", "no", "yes", "we'd be stuck" and "several", a managed back office is probably worth a conversation.

## Start small

You don't have to hand over everything. Start with **an extra set of hands**, where our specialist takes one job off your plate, like the inbox, the invoices or the data entry, quoted per job. Add more later if it's working.
