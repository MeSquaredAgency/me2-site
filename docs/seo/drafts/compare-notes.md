# Competitor fact notes (checked 25 October 2026)

## Virtual Coworker

Source: [virtualcoworker.com.au](https://www.virtualcoworker.com.au/) and its [pricing and savings guide](https://virtualcoworker.com.au/pricing-savings-guide/), fetched 25 Oct 2026.

What their site says:

- Helps Australian businesses hire dedicated virtual assistants based in the Philippines.
- "Supporting Businesses Since 2011."
- Sydney address shown: Level 8, 11 York Street, Sydney NSW 2000.
- Model: dedicated staff, 20 to 40 hours a week (minimum 20), working the client's preferred hours. A recruitment specialist sources and vets candidates; the client interviews and tests them.
- Pricing: published "hourly rates starting at", in AUD, described as inclusive with no extra fees and no recruitment fees. Examples from the pricing page: virtual assistant from $8/hr, executive assistant from $10/hr, bookkeeper from $11/hr, accountant from $12/hr, social media manager from $11/hr, digital marketing manager from $14/hr. Pricing depends on job description, hiring criteria, part- or full-time, and the candidate's history.
- Billing: card (with surcharge) or direct debit.
- Support: a Client Relationship Manager for the client and a Success Manager for the assistant.
- Monitoring: time logging, "real-time screenshots every 10 minutes" and daily activity reports.
- AI: says its assistants use AI to support their workflows.
- Service pages for virtual assistants, executive assistants, customer service, tech support, recruitment, lead generation, social media, digital marketing, web development and bookkeeping/accounting.
- Not found on the pages checked: notice period, minimum term, security certifications.

Fair contrasts a future page could draw (models, not quality):

- Their model: the client directs a dedicated staff member, billed by the hour. Me²'s model: a managed service, quoted per business or per job, with a dedicated main contact and specialists per field; Me² checks the work.
- Their location: Philippines. Me²: Phnom Penh, Cambodia (see the Cambodia vs Philippines guide for the time-zone and English trade-offs).
- They publish rates; Me² quotes.
- Productivity screenshots are part of their offer; Me² has no equivalent claim. Don't describe this as good or bad.

## Cloudstaff

- [Home page](https://cloudstaff.com/): connects clients with talent in the Philippines, Colombia, India and Kenya; lists ISO 27001, ISO 9001 and PCI-DSS, and GDPR and HIPAA compliance. Page title claims "1,000+ companies".
- [Australia page](https://www.cloudstaff.com/au/): also lists SOC 1 and SOC 2.
- [History page](https://www.cloudstaff.com/why-cloudstaff/cloudstaff-history/): founded in 2005 by Lloyd Ernst; Philippines operations began in 2010; cites more than 6,000 staff across 34 countries (figures vary by page and date).
- Pricing: not confirmed. [CONFIRM: whether they publish rates]
- Notice period / minimum term: not confirmed.

Fair contrasts a future page could draw:
