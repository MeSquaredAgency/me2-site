# Use-case example: property management admin

*This page is an example of the kind of work Me² does. It isn't a list of clients, and property management isn't the only kind of business we work with.*

A property manager's day is meant to be about owners, tenants and properties. In practice, much of it goes on admin: arrears reminders, lease paperwork, chasing tradespeople, booking inspections and keeping on top of a shared inbox that never empties.

Me² takes that admin off the property management team. [CONFIRM: "We already do this for an Australian agency with more than one office."] A trained Me² operations specialist with property management experience owns the work, AI agents set up for the agency do the drafting and the volume, and anything that needs a licensed agent's decision stays with the agent.

## What the work looks like

**Rental arrears.** We track arrears every day, keep a follow-up schedule for each tenancy and send reminders in the agency's voice. Where a breach or termination notice may be needed, we prepare it for the property manager to review and sign off. We never issue legal notices on our own.

**Leases, renewals and rent reviews.** Lease documentation, renewal offers and rent review paperwork prepared from the agency's templates and the tenancy records, checked against the source, and ready for the property manager to approve and send.

**Maintenance coordination.** Requests logged from tenants, quotes and approvals sought from owners within the agency's limits, tradespeople booked and followed up, and the job closed off in the system with the invoice attached.

**Inspections.** Routine and ingoing/outgoing inspections scheduled, entry notices prepared within the required notice periods, and reports checked for completeness and accuracy before they go to the owner.

**The shared inbox.** Enquiries sorted and prioritised, routine questions answered, and the urgent ones (a burst pipe on a Saturday) passed to the right person straight away.

**Reporting and records.** Arrears lists, vacancy and maintenance summaries prepared each week, and records kept up to date in the property management software.

## Approval limits, written down

Property management runs on licensing rules and on trust between owners, tenants and agents. So before we start, we write down with the agency what Me² does on its own, what needs a property manager's approval, and what only the licensee can do. All work is done within those agreed procedures and approval limits. Tasks that need an authorised agent's approval stay subject to it. [CONFIRM state-by-state wording]

Every process is written down and belongs to the agency. If the property manager is on leave, or a Me² team member is away, nothing stops.

## Why a dedicated person matters here

Tenancies have history. A tenant who is always a week late but always pays needs a different touch from a new arrears case. The agency gets one dedicated Me² person as its main contact, who learns the portfolio, the owners' preferences and how the agency likes things done. They bring in our finance and admin specialists where needed and report back like part of the team.

## People backed by AI, never AI on its own

AI agents draft the reminders, pre-fill the paperwork and summarise the inbox. A person checks every name, date, address and amount against the property management system before anything is sent to a tenant or an owner. If something goes wrong, the agency hears it from us. We fix it at no charge and update the process.

## Working hours and replies

Our team works from Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Our people start early to line up with Australian business hours,[^hours] and we usually reply within 2 hours, evenings and weekends included. Cambodia is three hours behind AEST and four hours behind AEDT.[^tz]

## Software

We work in the agency's existing property management software, inbox and document tools, so the team doesn't change systems. [CONFIRM: platforms Me² works in today]

## Data and privacy

Tenant and owner information is personal information, and an agency that sends it offshore needs to think about the Australian Privacy Principles, including APP 8 on cross-border disclosure. Only the Me² people doing the agency's work can see its data. Each has their own login, only to the systems their job needs, and we remove it when the work ends. The data is never used to train AI models. See our guide: [Is it safe to outsource admin offshore?](/guides/offshore-data-privacy/)

## Starting small

Many teams start with one job, such as arrears follow-up or the maintenance inbox, as **an extra set of hands**, quoted per job. Others hand over the whole property management admin as part of **the whole back office**, quoted for the business as one monthly fee billed in advance. Back-office work is month to month with 30 days' notice either way, and set-up is paid up front.

If you run a different kind of business, the same approach applies. See [admin and operations](/services/admin/).
