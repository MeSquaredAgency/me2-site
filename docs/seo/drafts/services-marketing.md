# Marketing, social media and paid ads, kept going every week

Most established businesses know what good marketing looks like. The newsletter, the posts, the follow-up on last month's enquiries. What's missing is someone whose job it is to do it every week.

Me² runs your marketing as part of your back office. A trained Me² marketing specialist owns the work, AI agents set up for your business do the drafting, and nothing goes out until you've approved it. Each month you get a plain-English report on what was done and what came of it.

## What we take on

**Newsletters.** Written in your voice from what's happening in your business, built in your email tool and sent on schedule once you've said yes.

**Social media.** Posts drafted on schedule in your voice, for you to approve. We keep a simple calendar so you can see what's coming, and we can reply to comments and messages under your rules.

**Paid ads.** Campaigns set up, watched and reported on. We check spend against results, pause what isn't working and tell you why. [CONFIRM: platforms] Your ad spend goes to the platform from your own account, so you can see every dollar. [CONFIRM]

**Website updates.** New pages, service changes, team updates and fixes made on your existing site. If you need a new one, we design and build websites too.

**Enquiry follow-up.** Leads from your forms and ads followed up and logged in your CRM, so marketing spend turns into conversations.

**A monthly report.** What went out, what people did with it, what we'd change next month. No vanity charts.

## Drafted for your approval

Marketing speaks for your business, so you stay in control. We write down your voice first: how you describe what you do, the words you'd never use, what you can and can't promise. Every post, email and ad is drafted from that guide and checked by your Me² specialist before it reaches you. You approve it, then it goes out. Over time, you can tell us which kinds of post no longer need your sign-off.

We don't invent reviews, results or statistics for you, and we won't publish claims you can't back up. That's your reputation, and it's ours too.

## People first

Me² is trained people backed by AI agents, never the other way round. AI helps us draft and do the volume. A person decides what's right for your business, checks it, and answers for it. Your dedicated Me² person is your main contact and brings in our marketing specialist, so you're not briefing a different freelancer each month.

## How it works

1. **Send us a short note** about what you want marketing to do for you.
2. **We write your voice guide and a simple monthly plan** with you.
3. **We work in your tools.** Your social accounts, your email platform, your ad accounts and your website. You keep ownership of all of them.
4. **We report monthly**, and your Me² person is there in between, usually replying within 2 hours.

We can start within 2 weeks of you saying yes.

## Start small or hand over the lot

Start with **an extra set of hands**, such as the monthly newsletter or the social calendar, quoted per job. Or hand over **the whole back office**, marketing alongside admin, finance and customer service, quoted for your business as one monthly fee billed in advance.

Month to month with 30 days' notice either way. Set-up is part of your quote and paid up front. Your accounts, data and written processes stay yours.

## Common questions

**Do you need access to our social and ad accounts?**
Yes, through your own accounts, with each Me² person on their own login and only to what their job needs. We remove access when the work ends.

**Will you post without asking?**
No. Everything is drafted for your approval until you tell us otherwise.

**Can you promise results?**
No one honestly can. We'll tell you what we're trying, report what happened and change course when something isn't working.
