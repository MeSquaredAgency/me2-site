# Admin and operations, handled

Your best person spent Friday building a report. The people who should be selling and looking after customers are stuck in spreadsheets and inboxes instead.

Me² runs the admin and the day-to-day operations behind your front line. You get one dedicated Me² person as your main contact, with our admin and operations specialists behind them. AI agents set up around your business do the drafting and the volume. A person owns every task and checks it before it leaves.

## What we take on

**The inbox.** We sort the shared inbox, answer the routine emails in your voice, forward what needs you and make sure nothing sits unanswered. You see a short list of what's waiting on you, not three hundred unread messages.

**Data entry.** New jobs, contacts, orders, timesheets, stock counts: entered into your systems accurately and on time, and checked against the source.

**Scheduling.** Appointments, bookings, reminders and diary juggling, so the people who do the work turn up in the right place.

**Client intake.** New enquiries logged, the details you need gathered, the paperwork sent and the client set up in your systems before your team picks it up.

**Quote follow-up.** Quotes that went out and heard nothing back get a polite follow-up on a schedule, and you see which ones are warm.

**Filing and records.** Documents named, filed and findable. Reports prepared on the day you need them.

## Every process written down

You hired for it. They left, and took the process with them. Every new hire started from scratch.

Before we take on a task, we write down how your business does it. Every repeat task becomes a written process, so nothing stops if your person is ever away, and the process belongs to you. If you ever leave, you take it with you.

## How it works

1. **Send us a short note** about what's eating your week. We'll tell you whether it's a fit.
2. **We map the work** and write the processes down with you.
3. **We set it up around your tools.** Usually you don't change software. We work in your inbox, your CRM and your job system. If anything would need to change, we'll say so first.
4. **Your Me² person reports back** and is the one you message when something comes up. Replies usually within 2 hours, evenings and weekends included.

We can start within 2 weeks of you saying yes.

## Start small or hand over the lot

Don't need a whole team? Start with **an extra set of hands**: our specialist takes one thing off your plate, like the inbox or the data entry. Quoted per job.

Or hand over **the whole back office**: admin and operations alongside finance, customer service and marketing, run by your dedicated Me² person. Quoted for your business, as one monthly fee billed in advance.

Back-office work runs month to month with 30 days' notice either way. Set-up is part of your quote and paid up front. You keep your data, your logins and your written processes.

## Common questions

**Is it people or AI doing the work?**
People, backed by AI. AI agents set up for your business do the drafting and the volume, and a person checks it before it reaches you. We don't sell AI on its own.

**Who can see our data?**
Only the Me² people doing your work, each with their own login to only the systems their job needs. We remove it when the work ends.

**What if your team gets something wrong?**
We fix it at no charge, tell you what happened, and update the written process so it doesn't happen again.
